# ApexGear Direct - Store Policies & Product Specifications

## 1. General Return and Refund Policy
- Return Window: Customers may request a return within exactly 15 calendar days from the verified date of carrier delivery.
- Eligibility Conditions: Items must be in brand-new, unused condition, with all original tags attached and in original packaging. Proof of purchase (Order ID or receipt) is required.
- Late Returns: Return requests submitted after 15 calendar days from delivery are strictly non-refundable and will be rejected. No exceptions or store credits are granted for late return requests.
- Refund Processing: Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days following warehouse inspection. A flat $4.50 restocking fee applies to non-defective remorse returns.

## 2. Product Warranty & Coverage
- Warranty Duration: ApexGear provides a 1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty on all electronic devices, bags, and tactical gear from the date of initial purchase.
- Covered Defects: Hardware malfunctions, seam tears, broken zipper teeth, defective buckles, and internal wiring failures resulting from normal, intended use.
- Exclusions: The warranty explicitly does not cover damage caused by accidents, water immersion beyond rated IPX standards, cosmetic wear and tear, animal damage, or unauthorized modifications and repairs.
- Claims Procedure: Customers must provide digital photos of the defect, serial number, and order receipt to warranty@apexgear.example.com.

## 3. Shipping Options & Delivery Windows
- Standard Ground Shipping: 3 to 5 business days transit time. Cost is $5.99; complimentary free shipping applies to domestic orders exceeding $50.00 pre-tax.
- Expedited Air Shipping: 2 business days transit time. Fixed flat fee of $14.99 nationwide.
- Priority Overnight: Guaranteed next-business-day delivery by 3:00 PM local time for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST. Cost is $29.99.
- International Delivery: Currently available only to Canada and the United Kingdom. Customs duties, taxes, and import fees are the sole responsibility of the recipient.

## 4. Price Match Guarantee
- ApexGear matches the advertised price of identical in-stock items from certified authorized retailers within 7 days of purchase.
- The item must share identical SKU, color, and model year. Clearance, liquidation, refurbished, open-box, and auction listings are ineligible for price matching.
